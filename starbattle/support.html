Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application WarriorsDoku ("the App") handles user information. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible while providing a simple and enjoyable puzzle experience.

1. Information We Collect

Local gameplay data: WarriorsDoku stores puzzle progress, completed levels, game settings, timer preferences, hint usage, and display preferences locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the game's functionality and allow you to continue your progress.

No personal information required: The App does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or account credentials.

2. Offline Gameplay

WarriorsDoku is designed to be playable without an internet connection. Your puzzle progress and game preferences are stored locally on your device.

No account is required to play the game.

3. Analytics and Advertising

The App may use third-party advertising or analytics services, such as Google AdMob, to support development and improve the overall user experience.

These services may collect information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Anonymous advertising identifiers
  • Basic app usage information

We do not directly access or control information collected by third-party service providers.

You can manage advertising and privacy preferences through your device settings where supported.

4. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay progress, settings, and completed puzzles are stored locally on your device.

The App does not upload your puzzle progress or personal information to our own external servers.

While we take reasonable measures to protect information, no electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

WarriorsDoku is designed to be suitable for users of all ages. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that personal information has been unintentionally collected from a child, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for advertising or analytics. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

This Privacy Policy applies only to WarriorsDoku. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party services used by the App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
