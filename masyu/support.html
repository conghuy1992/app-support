About Masyu: Pearl Puzzle

Masyu: Pearl Puzzle is a relaxing logic puzzle based on creating a single continuous loop through a grid. Every puzzle can be solved using deduction alone—no guessing required.

White pearls require the loop to travel straight through the pearl and turn immediately before or after it. Black pearls require the loop to turn on the pearl and continue straight through the neighboring cells.

Featuring clean visuals, intuitive controls, unlimited logical challenges, and smooth gameplay, Masyu: Pearl Puzzle is perfect for both beginners and experienced logic puzzle fans.

FAQ

Q: Is Masyu: Pearl Puzzle free to play?
A: Yes. The game is free to download and play. No account is required.

Q: Can I play offline?
A: Yes. All puzzles are fully playable offline, and your progress is saved locally on your device.

Q: How do I play?
A: Draw one continuous loop that passes through every pearl while following the rules for black and white pearls.

Q: Are the puzzles solvable without guessing?
A: Yes. Every puzzle has a unique solution that can be reached using logical deduction.

Q: Is my progress saved?
A: Yes. Puzzle progress and game settings are stored locally on your device.
