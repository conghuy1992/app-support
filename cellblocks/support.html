About Shikaku: CellBlock Puzzle

Shikaku: CellBlock Puzzle is a relaxing and challenging grid-based logic puzzle. Your goal is to divide the grid into rectangles so that each region contains exactly one number, and the size of the rectangle matches that number.

Each number represents how many cells its rectangle must cover. Rectangles cannot overlap and must fill the entire grid. Every puzzle has a unique solution and can be solved using logic alone.

The game features clean visuals, smooth interaction, and a focus on pure puzzle-solving. Whether you're a casual player or a logic puzzle enthusiast, you'll find a satisfying challenge in every level.

FAQ

Q: Is Shikaku: CellBlock Puzzle free to play?
A: Yes. The game is free to download and play. No account is required.

Q: Can I play offline?
A: Yes. All puzzles can be played offline. Progress is stored locally on your device.

Q: How do I play?
A: Divide the grid into rectangles. Each rectangle must contain exactly one number, and the number must match the size of that rectangle.

Q: Are there multiple solutions?
A: No. Each puzzle has a unique solution that can be solved using logic.

Q: Is my progress saved?
A: Yes. Your progress and settings are saved locally on your device.
